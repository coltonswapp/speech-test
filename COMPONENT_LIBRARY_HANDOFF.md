## Kanji quiz

Use the decomposition intro’s 22 pt bold centered prompt and its 200×200 pt glass word hero with furigana disabled. Put `入口` in that hero and use `「なんて読む？」` as the prompt. The native intro hardcodes an English meaning question and has a separate series eyebrow. The reel supplies its own prompt and can omit the eyebrow. Use the combined card’s centered 22 pt semibold meaning treatment for `いりぐち · entrance`, borrowing its meaning-only presentation. Keep the same hero and glyph bounds across every frame, with the answer on a separate line outside the plate. Native intro and combined slides independently center their stacks, and combined enables ruby. Switching between those full templates would move the word and expose the reading above it.

Give the countdown and answer a reserved slot whose height stays fixed. The iOS slide counter is static deck progress, so the changing numerals and frame-timed answer are new. Use the approved reel mix from frame 0. I verified that its PCM matches the same 140 ms tap at 0, 1 and 2 seconds and the bundled 420 ms reveal at 3 seconds. The reveal file carries the stamp and chime together. Use unity playback with no extra fade to preserve the baked mix and tails. Individual stamp/chime gains and fades are embedded and were not verified. Use the premix or schedule the component files, since layering both would double the cues. [Verified premix](../shizen-reels/out/kanji-quiz-sfx/countdown-and-reveal-v2.wav) · [Tap](../shizen-reels/out/kanji-quiz-sfx/tick.wav) · [Reveal](../shizen-reels/out/kanji-quiz-sfx/reveal.wav) This example uses hard cuts and a two-second answer hold as proposed choices. The source supplies no entrance or stamp spring parameters.

```ts
const quiz = {
  id: "kanji-reading-iriguchi",
  composition: {width: 1080, height: 1920, fps: 30, durationInFrames: 150},
  prompt: "「なんて読む？」",
  expression: "入口",
  answer: {kana: "いりぐち", gloss: "entrance", separator: " · "},
  hero: {style: "glass", glow: "yellow", widthPt: 200, heightPt: 200, ruby: false},
  countdown: [{from: 0, value: 3}, {from: 30, value: 2}, {from: 60, value: 1}],
  revealFrame: 90,
  audio: {
    sourceInReelRepo: "out/kanji-quiz-sfx/countdown-and-reveal-v2.wav",
    fromFrame: 0,
    cueFrames: {taps: [0, 30, 60], stampAndChime: 90},
    volume: 1,
  },
} as const;
```

Render one React tree throughout. Frames 0–29, 30–59 and 60–89 show 3, 2 and 1 respectively. Frame 90 removes the numeral and reveals the answer through frame 149. Derive visibility directly from the Remotion frame. Check the hero's pixel bounds on frames 0, 89 and 90. The prompt can persist through the answer in this example.

### Worked trace · dictionary-backed full decomposition

The [catalog fixture](shizen/Experiments/SlideComponentShowcaseViewController.swift#L337) resolves `電話` using `JMDictStore.shared.entries(forSurface:)` and `KanjiDecompositionWord.make(from:)`. The production decomposition list uses the same model. I verified that selecting 一人 in Device Hub opens the live five-page full slideshow, and recorded the page-control transition. The list passes the selected word to `KanjiDecompositionPagerViewController.init(word:)`. In full format the pager builds closures for the prompt plate, one breakdown each for 一 and 人 (電 and 話 in the gallery fixture), the equation teaser and the combined answer. Each breakdown resolves gloss/readings from KANJIDIC and other words from JMdict. The final slide uses the entry’s first gloss unless a session override supplies a nonempty replacement. The pager calls `prepareCard` to apply stored badge offsets on preview and export. Choosing short format rebuilds the sequence. [Live intro](component-references/KanjiDecompositionPagerViewController-live-intro.png) · [Model](shizen/Experiments/KanjiDecompositionModels.swift) · [Sequencing and prepare closure](shizen/Experiments/KanjiDecompositionPagerViewController.swift#L93) · [Layout store](shizen/Experiments/KanjiDecompositionBadgeLayout.swift)

`UIPageViewController` scrolls horizontally under a manual swipe. Page controls request a native animated forward/reverse transition. Transition completion updates the index and installs editor gestures. Slide content itself is configured in its end state. There are no per-card timeline triggers, delays, explicit easing/spring values, soundtrack cues, gains or fades in this path. A 0.45 s long press is an editor gesture threshold, and badge pan follows pointer displacement. Successful photo saves trigger UIKit notification haptic feedback. Neither is a slide audio event. [Paging callbacks](shizen/Experiments/ExperimentSlideshow.swift#L408) · [Badge editing](shizen/Experiments/KanjiDecompositionPagerViewController.swift#L270) · [Save callback](shizen/Experiments/ExperimentSlideshow.swift#L300)

Current slide or All slides rebuilds each selected card from its factory, reapplies preparation, hosts it briefly in a window, lays out and uses `drawHierarchy(afterScreenUpdates: true)` into an opaque @3× image. The app saves UIImages to Photos. Feed is 360×450 pt → 1080×1350 px, square 360×360 → 1080×1080 and Story 360×640 → 1080×1920. The preview scales the authored canvas uniformly to fit 16 pt side/top gutters and controls clearance +16 pt at bottom, with a 1 pt viewfinder border and 2 pt corner. The export uses canvas bounds without that editor chrome. ## Rendering and dependency notes

- **Coordinate model.** Use a 360×640 artboard with a top-left transform of 3, or multiply every authored point by 3 on a 1080×1920 composition. Use one approach throughout. The live preview’s safe-area and footer bounds are editor layout. Use canvas-edge distances for watermark and guards, with any social-platform UI insets defined separately by the reel. Simulate Auto Layout’s priorities with fixed plate widths, explicit min/max dimensions and measured text. Keep the card mostly unclipped for badge overlap, with clip at the composition edge.
- **Palette and glass.** Light page RGB (.949,.949,.949), white surface and RGB (.88,.88,.88) borders. Dark page and raised card use grouped backgrounds, with 12% white border. Solid and badge borders are 1 pt. Badge text defaults to (.68,.48,0) light or (.92,.74,.18) dark. Glass layers are clipped radial glow, native liquid-glass effect, then content. Bottom glow spans width 1.0 and height .12, offset (0,−5.88), .39 opacity, blur 14, halo opacity .3 and nominal glow radius 36. Approximate continuous corners, translucency, tint and hairline in CSS against the exported stills. Compositor parity is unverified. [Palette](shizen/Experiments/ExperimentPalette.swift) · [Glass](shizen/UI/UnderglowGlassView.swift) · [Glow configuration](shizen/Dialogue/DialogueBubbleUnderglowConfiguration.swift#L240) · [Effect and compatibility fallback](InteractionKit/Sources/InteractionKit/LiquidGlassEffectView.swift)
- **Typography and inputs.** iOS system fonts, Japanese fallback, and Core Text ruby are OS-provided. Supply licensed web fonts and deterministic fallbacks, set `lang="ja"`, use Japanese line breaking with ruby where authored, and measure the reading and base together. Give translations their own wrap/scale rules. Particular fallback fonts in the simulator were not measured. Prefill readings in the render payload to preserve segmentation when a dictionary lookup fails or produces alternative readings. [Ruby builder and label](shizen/Dictionary/JapaneseFuriganaBuilder.swift) · [Tokenizer](shizen/Dictionary/JapaneseTokenizer.swift)
- **Dictionary data.** [KANJIDIC bundle](shizen/kanjidic.sqlite), [KANJIDIC store](shizen/Dictionary/KanjidicStore.swift), [JMdict store](shizen/Dictionary/JMDictStore.swift) and the [JMdict build script](scripts/build_jmdict.py) for `shizen/jmdict.sqlite`. Preserve applicable dictionary attribution and verify upstream reuse terms before shipping data or derivatives. For a quiz payload with curated strings, the renderer needs neither database at runtime.
- **Icons, images and audio.** Templates use labels, vector borders and literal signs such as ↕ and +. The native editor renders SF Symbols for controls like the gallery paintbrush, Play All and export. Those are editor chrome. The watermark is a text label. The traced slides require no standalone image or audio files. The approved quiz WAVs live in the reel’s generated output directory. Resolve or stage the verified mix as a render input for Remotion. The handoff references the original audio in the sibling project. The reel’s [existing narrated carousel countdown](../shizen-reels/src/carousel/CarouselVideoScenes.tsx#L340) is a precedent for frame-dependent visibility, with different numbers and timing from this quiz.
- **Motion.** Native swipe travel and page-control scrolling use UIKit timing. No numeric timing or spring constants are authored for those transitions. The clips depict interaction from one simulator run. If using Remotion springs for a new design, specify the duration, damping, stiffness, mass and animated properties as reel decisions and evaluate them from the frame clock.
