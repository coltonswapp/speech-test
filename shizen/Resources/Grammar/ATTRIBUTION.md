# Grammar content attribution

Example sentences and vocabulary in `n5.grammar.json` may be seeded from [JLPT Sensei](https://jlptsensei.com) N5 grammar lessons during editorial import. Teaching copy in the app is written for Shizen and should not be assumed to match third-party site wording.

Before shipping a build that redistributes example sentences at scale, confirm permission or licensing with JLPT Sensei.

Imported HTML is not stored in this repository—only processed JSON.
